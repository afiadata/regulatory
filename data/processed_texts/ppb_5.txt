Dear Readers,
Welcome to the 23rd edition of the PPB Newsletter! In 
this issue, we offer a glimpse into the dedicated work 
being done by the Pharmacy and Poisons Board (PPB) 
and its partners to ensure quality, safety, and efficiency in 
pharmaceutical operations.
Through the articles presented, we shed light on the 
transformative developments taking place in the 
industry. From regulatory reforms aimed at bolstering 
local manufacturing to collaborations between Kenya 
and the World Health Organization (WHO) for enhanced 
regulatory systems, we explore the initiatives shaping the 
future of pharmaceuticals.
We value your feedback and contributions to make our 
newsletter even more informative and engaging. If 
you have any suggestions, comments, or would like to 
contribute an article for future editions, please reach out 
to us at pr@pharmacyboardkenya.org. Your input is vital 
in shaping the content and ensuring that we continue to 
provide you with valuable insights.
Thank you for your continued support, and enjoy 
exploring the informative content in this edition of the 
PPB Newsletter!
Looking forward with you 
mind on our transformative 
agenda
Judy

The Chairman of the Pharmacy

Have you come across any suspected poor quality health products, or 
experienced any suspected side effects following the use of health 
products including medicines and vaccines? You are encouraged to 
report to the Pharmacy and Poisons Board through either of the 
following:
a. Dial *271# on your phone
 
(free on Safaricom, Telkom and Airtel mobile networks)
b. Download and install mPvERS mobile application on your smart 
phone from Google Play Store or Apple Store
c. Login into: https://pv.pharmacyboardkenya.org/padrs/add
d. Call 0795743049
You don’t need to be certain,
just be suspicious!!!
